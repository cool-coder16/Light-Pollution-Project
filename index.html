This graph shows the average light pollution above San Jose by month. You can click the dates in the bottom to set the range, between January 2012 to September 2026.

Light pollution is important, because it blocks us from seeing the stars. If you go to places with almost no light pollution, you can see things which are invisible near cities. Also, it harms migratory birds, who are more likely to stop near places with more skyglow, leading them to stop in cities, harming their usual flightpath, and sometimes even getting them stuck there.
